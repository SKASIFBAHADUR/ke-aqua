KE AQUA FARMS

Website Content & Development Brief

1. Website Objective

The website should establish KE Aqua Farms as a practical and science-driven aquaculture platform—not simply an aqua products dealership.

The website should communicate four strengths immediately:

Practical Farming + Scientific Monitoring + Technology + Farmer/Entrepreneur Development

The website may take professional inspiration from AquaExchange in presentation quality, but the design, wording and identity must be completely original.

Primary Website Brand

26. DESIGN DIRECTION

Tell your designer:

Desktop-first professional appearance but fully mobile responsive.

Style:

Modern + Scientific + Agricultural + Premium + Clean

Avoid:

Too many animations
Too many colours
Generic shrimp stock images
Crowded text
Cheap-looking gradients
Unnecessary pop-ups
Fake statistics
Fake testimonials
Overuse of certificates/logos

Use large actual farm photography and generous spacing.
