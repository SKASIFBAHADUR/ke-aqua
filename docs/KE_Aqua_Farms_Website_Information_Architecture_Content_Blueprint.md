# KE Aqua Farms — Website Information Architecture & Content Blueprint

**Status:** Source-grounded production specification  
**Date:** 01 October 2026

## 1. Executive Summary & Visual Direction
The platform should present an integrated aquaculture and seafood ecosystem—not a simple aqua-products dealership—centered on practical farming, scientific monitoring, suitable technology, practical learning, farmer/entrepreneur development, responsible aquaculture and seafood value addition.

**Brand architecture requiring explicit resolution:** the developer master brief uses **KE AQUA GROUP** as the primary digital brand, while the KE Aqua Farms profile positions **KE AQUA FARMS** as the working farm/training/demonstration brand.

**Visual system:** modern + scientific + agricultural + premium + clean; deep navy, white, aqua/blue and controlled green; authentic farm/lab/training imagery; generous spacing; restrained motion; no literal key-symbol dependence.

## 3. Page Blueprint
### Home
Hero → Who We Are → Aqua Ecosystem → Real Working Farms → Aqua Pulse/Science → Practical Learning → Technology → Seed-to-Harvest → Responsible Aquaculture → Case Studies/Impact → Knowledge Hub → Stories → Founder → Media → Visit/Training CTA → Contact/Footer.

### About KE Aqua Group
Mission, vision, philosophy, history/story, ecosystem diagram and group-to-division relationships.

### Our Businesses
KE Aqua Farms = farming/demonstration/learning; Kamatham Enterprises = inputs/health/support; Aqua Pulse = lab/science/technical support; KE Coastal = processing/value addition.

### Our Farms
Real farm, infrastructure, crop journey, records and farm-visit CTA.

### Aqua Pulse Lab
Water-quality monitoring, microscopy, microbiology/Vibrio and periodic monitoring only where actually available; sample → test → interpret → decision; disclaimer.

### Farm Technology
Auto feeders, farm monitoring, water monitoring, IoT/sensors, aeration, micro/nano bubbles, lab monitoring and records using **FARM PROBLEM → TECHNOLOGY → OUR PRACTICE → OBSERVATION → POTENTIAL BENEFIT**.

### Seed Selection
Hatchery evaluation → observation → stress assessment → screening → selection → transport → acclimatization → stocking; no guaranteed crop claims.

### Practical Training
KE Practical Aquaculture Learning Farm; practical-first training; programs, modules, skills, method, outcomes and disclaimer. Source-supported balance: 20% explanation / 70% practical / 10% review.

### Farmer Development
Inclusive learning, 1-day/2-day/3-day/7-day/1-month/3-month ladder, My Training Pond/Pond Passport, skill passport, assessment and Visitor → Learner → Farm Worker → Pond Operator → Farm Manager → Farmer.

### Aquaculture Entrepreneurship
Hatchery → Feed → Farming → Healthcare → Laboratory → Technology → Equipment → Processing → Cold Chain → Marketing → Retail → Export. No investment advice/return guarantees.

### Responsible Aquaculture
Biosecurity, water management, responsible inputs, antibiotic awareness, food safety, worker safety, environmental responsibility, traceability and women/scientific farmer awareness.

### Case Studies
Documented farm/crop cases only; include farm, pond, dates, seed, feed, technology, monitoring, production, verified metrics, challenges, corrective action and learning.

### Knowledge Hub
Farm practices, seed, water, health, feeding, technology, biosecurity, cost, industry updates, interviews, videos and case studies. Pattern: Problem → Observation → Practical Experience → Learning.

### Media
TV, podcasts, demonstrations, meetings, training, exhibitions, college programs, awareness and publications.

### Founder
Kamatham Emmanuel; Aquaculture Practitioner | Farmer | Entrepreneur | Technical Trainer; farm-first story and founder philosophy.

### Registrations & Credentials
Separate entity-specific GST/NFDP/Aqua Trace/processing credentials. Never imply one entity's registration certifies the group. Do not publish ASC Certified until officially issued.

### KE Coastal
Processing/value addition and market development when operational and documented.

### Contact
LET'S TALK AQUACULTURE; structured enquiry form covering identity, audience, interest and message; WhatsApp/Maps only after exact details are confirmed.

## 4. Shared Global Components
Header: clear navigation, primary CTA, desktop grouping/dropdowns and mobile drawer.  
Footer: entity-aware business links, services/programs, contact/WhatsApp if confirmed, social links if confirmed, location/map if approved, legal links and credentials.

Reusable UI: hero, cards, process flows, value-chain diagrams, technology cards, verified metric cards, article cards, enquiry forms and responsive media galleries.

## 5. User Journeys
- Farmer: Farm → Monitoring/Lab → Support → Enquiry
- Aspiring farmer: Farm Visit → Training → Farmer Development → Enquiry
- Student/graduate: Knowledge → Farm Visit → Training
- Entrepreneur: Value Chain → Farm Exposure → Training → Business Enquiry
- Investor: Business Understanding → Farm Exposure → Independent Evaluation
- Professional: Lab/Technology → Technical Enquiry

## 6. Governance
Never invent tests, products, prices, stock, brands, dealer status, certifications, statistics, testimonials, service claims or performance outcomes. Keep all four entities distinct. Use only verified credentials and authentic visuals.

## 7. Open Decisions
Primary CTA; audience priority; separate-page grouping; training application/payment/date/availability; lab booking/pricing/uploads/reports/login; Knowledge Hub CMS; lead destination/storage; WhatsApp/social links; exact locations/maps; languages; legal documents; commercial status of the 16 detailed services; exact scope/pricing/availability; KE AQUA GROUP vs KE AQUA FARMS brand hierarchy.

## 8. Final Architecture Principle
**REAL FARM → SCIENCE & LAB → TECHNOLOGY → PRACTICAL LEARNING → FARMER DEVELOPMENT → ENTREPRENEURSHIP → RESPONSIBLE AQUACULTURE → SEAFOOD VALUE ADDITION → ENQUIRY**
